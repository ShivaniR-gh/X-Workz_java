About Princeton University

Founded in 1746, Princeton is a prestigious Ivy League university located in Princeton, New Jersey. It is known for academic excellence, innovation, and commitment to public service.

Princeton University is a private Ivy League research university located in Princeton, New Jersey. Founded in 1746, it's the fourth-oldest institution of higher education in the United States. Known for its strong undergraduate program and commitment to research, Princeton emphasizes a liberal arts education across humanities, arts, social sciences, natural sciences, and engineering.
